About Heron Atlas

Heron Atlas (Chinese name 鹭图) is a continuously updated index of macro observation. We start from official primary releases, follow changes in the economy and in policy, organise events in half-hourly selections, and link the threads with reviewed opinion articles. A heron stands still in shallow water and watches before it moves; an atlas is a map, a way of drawing scattered facts into lines that can be checked.

What we provide

  • Titles, sources and publication times of official releases, so readers can go back to the original material.
  • A half-hourly event index with data cut-off times and processing status.
  • Macro opinion articles, published after review (in Chinese).

Scope and caveats

The site is read-only. Content comes from scheduled public snapshots; collection can be delayed or miss items, and the time and status of each item are as marked on the page. Official releases, rule-based selections and research interpretation are different layers and do not verify one another.

Content is for information and research only. It is not investment advice and offers no trading. Links exist to trace the original source; rights in the material remain with the original publishers.

English pages

Our first reads are written in Chinese. On the English pages, titles that are not in English and our first reads are machine translations, checked automatically for numbers, names and leftover source text; they may still contain errors or omissions. The official original always prevails. An item appears in English once its translation has passed these checks.

Editorial note

The site is edited and maintained by its founder, He Ren, as an independent research record not affiliated with any institution. Articles are first organised with rule and model tools, which draft research and check each factual citation, and are then reviewed by the editor before publication; content marked as AI-assisted has been reviewed by a person. Corrections and comments on sources can be traced through the source links on each article page; contact details will be added in a later version.
