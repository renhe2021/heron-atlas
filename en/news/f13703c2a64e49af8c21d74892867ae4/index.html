Dong Lijuan, Chief Statistician of the Urban Department, National Bureau of Statistics, interprets October 2026 CPI and PPI data

国家统计局城市司首席统计师董莉娟解读2026年10月份CPI和PPI数据

Collected Nov 9, 2026 09:45 Beijing

First read

In October, the consumer price index (CPI) was flat month on month.

The CPI rose 0.7% year on year, with the increase down 0.2 percentage point from the previous month.

The core CPI, excluding food and energy prices, rose 0.8% year on year, with the increase down 0.2 percentage point from the previous month.

The month-on-month increase in the producer price index (PPI) for industrial products eased.
