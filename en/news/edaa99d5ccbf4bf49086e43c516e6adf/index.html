January–September 2026: profits of industrial enterprises above designated size nationwide up 15.5%

2026年1—9月份全国规模以上工业企业利润增长15.5%

Collected Oct 27, 2026 09:37 Beijing

First read

In January–September, industrial enterprises above designated size nationwide achieved total profits of 6,033.74 billion yuan, up 15.5% year on year.

State-controlled enterprises achieved total profits of 1,939.55 billion yuan, up 13.1% year on year; joint-stock enterprises achieved total profits of 4,672.76 billion yuan, up 20.8%.

Foreign-invested enterprises and those from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan achieved total profits of 1,337.14 billion yuan, up 0.5%.
