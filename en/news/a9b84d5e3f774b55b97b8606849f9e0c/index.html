Auction Result of Treasury Discount Bills on October 2, 2026

Published Oct 2, 2026 11:30 Beijing2026-10-02 03:30 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the tender results for the 2026 October 2 Treasury Discount Bills (Issue No. 1410).

The issue's issuance date is October 5, and its maturity date is January 12, 2027; the competitive bid amount was 11,710.50 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 2,698.48 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.6520 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.2875%, and the weighted average price was 99.6542, corresponding to a yield of 1.2793%.

The amount of non-competitive tender I for special participants in the government bond market was 801.50 billion yen.
