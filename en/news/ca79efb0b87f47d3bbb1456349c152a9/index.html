Bank of Japan Accounts (October 10)

Published Oct 14, 2026 9:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan published its balance sheet as of October 10, 2026, with total assets, total liabilities and net assets all at 624,632,707,190 thousand yen.

On the asset side, this includes Japanese government bonds of 503,634,998,862 thousand yen, loans (excluding the Deposit Insurance Corporation) of 68,415,800,000 thousand yen, and foreign currency assets of 12,031,877,130 thousand yen, among others.

On the liability side, this includes banknotes in circulation of 114,033,833,895 thousand yen and current deposits of 408,161,721,227 thousand yen.

The supplementary table shows a balance of 35,664,500,000 thousand yen for the fund-provisioning measure to stimulate bank lending under the loan support program, and a loan balance of 32,751,300,000 thousand yen for fund-provisioning operations, etc. based on pooled collateral.
