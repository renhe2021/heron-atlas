Signing of the Japan-Australia Joint Strategic Financing Memorandum of Cooperation and the Japan-Australia Strategic Investment Networking Forum (October 2, 2026)

Published Oct 5, 2026 11:00 Beijing

First read

The Government of Japan (represented by the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry) and the Government of Australia (represented by the Department of the Treasury and the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade) recently signed the Memorandum of Cooperation on Joint Strategic Financing.

The memorandum reflects the growing importance of strategic financing in advancing shared economic security objectives, and aims to strengthen cooperation between Japanese and Australian institutions and to leverage private-sector investment, expertise, and innovation.

The memorandum was signed at the inaugural Japan-Australia Strategic Investment Network Forum, which is part of the events commemorating the 50th anniversary of the signing of the Japan-Australia Basic Treaty of Friendship and Cooperation.

The forum brought together representatives of approximately 20 Japanese and Australian policy and financing institutions to deepen relationships, enhance mutual understanding, and discuss cooperation opportunities in strategic investment in the Indo-Pacific region.
