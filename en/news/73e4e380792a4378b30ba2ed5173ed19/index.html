CPMI publishes analysis on how central banks support payment system safety and efficiency and trust in money in the rapidly changing payments ecosystem

Published Oct 29, 2026 08:00 Beijing

First read

The Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI) of the Bank for International Settlements has published a new report.

The report analyses how central banks can support the safety and efficiency of payment systems and trust in money in a rapidly changing payment ecosystem.

The report examines how technological innovation, changing user needs and the emergence of new payment service providers and arrangements are changing payment systems.
