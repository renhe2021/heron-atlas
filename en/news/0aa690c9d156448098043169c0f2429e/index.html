Auction Result of 40-Year JGBs on September 29, 2026(For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Sep 29, 2026 14:15 Beijing2026-09-29 06:15 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of 40-year government bonds (No. 19) held on September 29, 2026 for JGB market special participants.

The competitive bid amount was 928.0 billion yen, the accepted amount was 299.7 billion yen, and the highest accepted yield was 4.125%.

The price corresponding to the highest accepted yield was 93.69 per 100 yen, and the allotment ratio at that yield level was 42.4153%.

The accepted amount for JGB market special participants in non-price competitive auction II was 25.3 billion yen.
