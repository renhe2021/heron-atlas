Announcement of 30-year JGBs to Be Issued in October 2026

Published Oct 1, 2026 09:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that it will issue 30-year JGBs in October 2026.

The auction date is October 8, 2026, the issue date is October 9, 2026, and the maturity date is September 20, 2056.

The issue size is approximately 600 billion yen.
