Announcement of 10-year Inflation-Indexed JGBs to Be Issued in November 2026

Published Nov 9, 2026 09:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the issuance arrangements for 10-year inflation-linked JGBs to be issued in November 2026.

The auction date for the bonds is November 16, 2026, the issue date is November 17, 2026, and the maturity date is March 10, 2036.

The issue size is approximately 250 billion yen, and this issuance is a reopening of the series issued in May 2026.

The auction method is a Dutch-style price-competitive auction, with bidding units of 0.05 yen.
