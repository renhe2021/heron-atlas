Auction Result of 30-Year JGBs on October 8, 2026

Published Oct 8, 2026 11:35 Beijing2026-10-08 03:35 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of the 92nd 30-year JGBs held on October 8, 2026.

The coupon rate for this issue is 4.2%, and the maturity date is September 20, 2056.

The competitive bid amount was 1,747.2 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 450.7 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 101.05 yen per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 4.121%; the weighted average price was 101.21 yen per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 4.109%.
