Auction Result of Treasury Discount Bills on October 16, 2026

Published Oct 16, 2026 11:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the tender results for the 2026 October 16 Treasury Discount Bills (No. 1413).

The issue date for these bills is October 19, and the maturity date is January 25, 2027; the total competitive bid amount was 10,869.10 billion yen, and the total amount accepted was 2,529.08 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.6560 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.2856%, and the weighted average price was 99.6574, corresponding to a yield of 1.2803%.

The amount of non-competitive tender I for special participants in the government bond market was 770.90 billion yen.
