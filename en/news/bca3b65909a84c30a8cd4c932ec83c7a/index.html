International Reserves/Foreign Currency Liquidity (as of the end of October 2026)

Published Nov 9, 2026 07:50 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that as of the end of October 2026, Japan's total reserve assets were 1,179,119 million US dollars, an increase of 723 million US dollars compared with the end of September 2026.

Among official reserve assets, foreign currency reserves were 985,228 million US dollars, of which securities were 834,555 million US dollars and deposits were 150,673 million US dollars; the IMF reserve position was 11,167 million US dollars, SDRs were 60,627 million US dollars, gold was 106,437 million US dollars (27.20 million troy ounces), and other reserve assets were 15,660 million US dollars.

Other foreign currency assets were 36,759 million US dollars, including loans to the Japan Bank for International Cooperation totaling 36,390 million US dollars.

Regarding scheduled short-term net outflows of foreign currency assets, the combined long positions in foreign currency against yen forwards and futures were 20,000 million US dollars; regarding contingent short-term net outflows, foreign currency contingent liabilities were -10,250 million US dollars.
