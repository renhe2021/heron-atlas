Statement by the Honorable KATAYAMA Satsuki, Governor of the International Monetary Fund and the World Bank Group for Japan(Bangkok ― October 16, 2026)

Published Oct 17, 2026 17:00 Beijing

First read

Japanese Finance Minister Katayama stated at the IMF and World Bank Annual Meetings that the global economy faces high uncertainty, including geopolitical tensions and the negative impact of trade restrictions on supply chains, and that the situation in the Middle East is adversely affecting global energy supply and logistics.

Japan is in a phase of transition to positive inflation and positive interest rates, and will maintain market confidence and achieve both economic growth and fiscal sustainability by steadily reducing the ratio of government debt to GDP.

Japan calls on the IMF to deepen empirical analysis of negative spillovers such as overcapacity caused by non-market policies and practices, and commits to continuing its voluntary contributions to the PRGT.

Japan commits 40 million US dollars to the Global Financing Facility (GFF) for maternal and child health and nutrition, and supports the World Bank in accelerating critical minerals investment through RISE+ and strengthening MIGA's guarantee functions.
