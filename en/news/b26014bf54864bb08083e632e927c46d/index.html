Auction Result of 2-Year JGBs on October 29, 2026

Published Oct 29, 2026 11:35 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of 2-year JGBs (No. 490) held on October 29, 2026.

The competitive bid amount in this auction was 8,539.2 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 2,118.4 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.975 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.912%; the weighted average price was 99.989 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.905%.

The accepted amount of non-competitive bids was 1.070 billion yen, and the accepted amount of the non-price competitive auction I for JGB market special participants was 680.1 billion yen.
