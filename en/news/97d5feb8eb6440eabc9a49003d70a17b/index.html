Frank Elderson: Effective supervision through timely remediation

Published Oct 6, 2026 21:00 Beijing

First read

The ECB's banking supervision arm is putting effectiveness and timely remediation at the core of its dialogue with banks, and stresses that simplified supervision and effective supervision reinforce rather than oppose each other.

As of end-2025, the stock of pending supervisory measures at significant banks rose to about 12,000, or about 100 per bank on average; in 2025, more measures were closed than newly added, by 1,200, and in 2026 the stock fell by a further 600.

The ECB began implementing a tiered approach in 2025, under which low-severity findings (F1) will be communicated only as supervisory observations and no longer generate measures, and the mandatory internal audit validation for low-severity internal model findings was abolished.

For material deficiencies that are not remediated in a timely manner, supervisors will make greater use of escalation tools including capital requirements, business restrictions and periodic penalties.
