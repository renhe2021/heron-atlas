Buy-back Results in September 2026

Published Oct 2, 2026 16:00 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the buyback results for 10-year inflation-linked government bonds for September 2026.

In September, bonds No. 22, 23, 24, 29 and 30 were bought back, with a total buyback amount of 15.1 billion yen.

The table also lists cumulative buyback data for April to June 2026, July and September, totaling 60.3 billion yen.

A note states that bond No. 31 was not included among the bonds eligible for buyback from April and July 2026.
