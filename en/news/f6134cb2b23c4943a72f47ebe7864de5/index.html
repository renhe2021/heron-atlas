Results of the 107th Opinion Survey on the General Public's Views and Behavior

Published Oct 13, 2026 12:30 Beijing2026-10-13 04:30 UTC

First read

The Bank of Japan released the results of the 107th Public Opinion and Behavior Survey on October 13, 2026, with the survey period from August 3 to September 4, 2026, and a valid response rate of 49.3%.

58.2% of respondents judged that current economic conditions had worsened compared with one year earlier, with a diffusion index (D.I.) of -53.0; 42.8% expected economic conditions to worsen over the next year, with a D.I. of -36.2.

68.9% of respondents said the current price level had "risen significantly" compared with one year earlier, and 34.5% expected prices to "rise significantly" and 54.1% to "rise slightly" one year ahead.

30.3% of respondents said they knew that price stability is one of the Bank of Japan's goals, 21.9% knew of the 2% price stability target, and a combined 43.5% said they had "confidence" or "some confidence" in the Bank of Japan.

InterpretationThe survey shows that public inflation expectations remain elevated and economic sentiment is on the pessimistic side; if this perception becomes entrenched, it could reinforce market expectations for the Bank of Japan to further tighten monetary policy and raise the policy rate, thereby providing support for the yen exchange rate.
