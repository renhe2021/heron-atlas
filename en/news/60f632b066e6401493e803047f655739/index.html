Central Government Debt(As of September 30, 2026)

Published Nov 10, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance published central government debt data as of September 30, 2026, including the balance of government bonds and borrowings and the balance of government-guaranteed debt.

The combined balance of government bonds and borrowings was 13,233,632 hundred million yen, down 233,201 hundred million yen from the end of the previous quarter and down 204,794 hundred million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year.

The balance of government bonds was 12,148,483 hundred million yen, of which ordinary bonds were 11,151,359 hundred million yen and short-term bonds were 361,996 hundred million yen, down 49,000 hundred million yen from the end of the previous quarter.

The balance of government-guaranteed debt was 262,983 hundred million yen, down 11,363 hundred million yen from the end of the previous quarter.
