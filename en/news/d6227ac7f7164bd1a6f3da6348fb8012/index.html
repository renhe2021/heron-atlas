Output Gap, Potential Growth Rate, and Labor Market Indicators

Published Oct 5, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan's Research and Statistics Department compiles and publishes estimates of the output gap and potential growth rate on a quarterly basis, which are used to assess economic and price conditions.

Given that labor supply constraints have intensified in recent years, the impact of labor input and labor market tightness on economic activity and price developments in labor-intensive industries has become increasingly significant, and the Bank of Japan has begun publishing labor market indicators on a quarterly basis.

These labor market indicators are considered suitable for supplementary monitoring of the output gap because they are correlated with wage and price forecasts, and they are published together with updated data on the output gap and potential growth rate.

Estimates of the output gap and potential growth rate may vary widely depending on the method and are subject to estimation error, so they need to be viewed with some latitude.
