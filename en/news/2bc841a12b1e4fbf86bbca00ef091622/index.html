Japan Customs and the Dutch Customs Exchange a Cooperation Document to Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

Published Nov 6, 2026 19:45 Beijing

First read

On November 5, 2026, the Customs and Tariff Bureau of Japan's Ministry of Finance and the Customs Administration of the Netherlands Ministry of Finance held talks, exchanging views on issues of mutual interest including combating illicit goods smuggling (including the use of advanced technologies such as AI), addressing cross-border e-commerce (including cooperation with platform operators), promoting public-private cooperation, and the use of advanced inspection equipment.

The two customs authorities exchanged a document aimed at strengthening cooperation, which identifies three priority areas of cooperation: (i) advanced technology and AI, (ii) cross-border e-commerce, and (iii) risk management and targeting, and provides for the two sides to hold regular expert-level meetings in these areas to promote information sharing and exchange of views.

The Netherlands customs delegation also visited facilities including the Haneda Customs Branch and the Kawasaki Overseas Mail Branch office, deepening its understanding of Japanese customs initiatives, and exchanged views with a commercial airline and other private-sector stakeholders on approaches to public-private cooperation.

The two customs authorities will further strengthen cooperation in the future by steadily advancing the initiatives under the cooperation document, building on the discussions at this meeting.
