Christine Lagarde: IMFC Statement

Published Oct 17, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

The global economy has shown resilience amid high uncertainty, but the Middle East conflict, trade tensions and AI investment pose complex effects on the growth and inflation outlook.

The euro-area economy achieved broad-based growth in the first half of 2026, and the September staff projections expect growth rates of 0.9%, 1.4% and 1.5% for 2026, 2027 and 2028 respectively.

Driven by a surge in energy inflation, headline inflation rose to 3.8% in September and core inflation edged up to 2.5%, and high energy prices are expected to keep headline inflation well above the target level until the first half of 2027.

The ECB Governing Council raised key rates by 25 basis points in September, noting that the energy shock is too large to ignore but that a moderate response to keep inflation controllable is appropriate.
