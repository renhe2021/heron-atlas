GDP (Advance Estimate), 3rd Quarter 2026

Published Oct 29, 2026 20:30 Beijing2026-10-29 12:30 UTC

First read

First-read data released by the US Bureau of Economic Analysis showed that US real gross domestic product grew at an annualized rate of 3.4% in the third quarter of 2026.

Real GDP grew 2.2% in the second quarter.

The main contributions to third-quarter real GDP growth came from increases in investment, consumer spending and government spending, partly offset by a decrease in exports.

Imports, which are a subtraction in the calculation of GDP, increased this quarter.
