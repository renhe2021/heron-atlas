Decision to Impose Definitive Anti-Dumping Duties on Nickel-added cold-rolled stainless steel coil, sheet and strip originating in or exported from the People’s Republic of China and the Separate Customs Territory of Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu

Published Oct 27, 2026 14:00 Beijing

First read

On October 27, 2026, the Japanese Cabinet adopted a cabinet order deciding to impose final anti-dumping duties on nickel-containing cold-rolled stainless steel coils, plates and strips originating in or exported from China and the Separate Customs Territory of Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu.

The investigation confirmed the existence of dumped imports and material injury to the domestic industry; the final anti-dumping duty rates are the same as the provisional rates, ranging from 3.6% to 42.1%, and the imposition period is from October 31, 2026 to October 30, 2031.

The cabinet order will be promulgated on October 30, 2026; the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry had conducted the investigation since July 22, 2025, and had imposed provisional anti-dumping duties since July 9, 2026.
