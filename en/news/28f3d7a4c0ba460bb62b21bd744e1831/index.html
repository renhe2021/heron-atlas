Piero Cipollone: Interview with Corriere della Sera

Published Oct 8, 2026 14:00 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone said the digital euro will not replace cash but will serve as a complement, will be free for individuals, and will support offline payments.

He expects the digital euro could be launched in 2029; the pilot project was launched in the summer, the first tests will begin next year, and the first payment will be completed in less than 12 months.

Of the nearly 60 applications to participate in the pilot received in May, 36 institutions have been selected; the relevant legislative text is expected to be ready before the end of the year.

He stressed that geopolitical factors have accelerated the project, and that Europe needs its own digital payments infrastructure to avoid dependence on external operators and to provide merchants with a lower-cost public payment alternative.
