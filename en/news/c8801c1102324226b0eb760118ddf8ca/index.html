Auction Result of 2-Year JGBs on September 30, 2026

Published Sep 30, 2026 11:35 Beijing2026-09-30 03:35 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of the 489th 2-year JGBs held on September 30, 2026.

The coupon rate for this issue is 2.0%, the total competitive bid amount was 8,344.8 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 2,145.3 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 100.055 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.971%; the weighted average price was 100.069 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.964%.

In non-price competitive auction I, the accepted amount for JGB market special participants was 654.0 billion yen.
