Announcement of Liquidity Enhancement Auction on October 22, 2026

Published Oct 15, 2026 09:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that it will hold a liquidity enhancement auction on October 22, 2026, with an issue date of October 23, 2026.

Eligible issues include existing 10-year JGBs No. 365 through No. 383, existing 20-year JGBs No. 132 through No. 162, and existing 30-year JGBs No. 6 through No. 27.

The offered amount for this auction is approximately 500 billion yen.
