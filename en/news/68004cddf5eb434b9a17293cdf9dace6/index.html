Announcement of 2-year JGBs to Be Issued in November 2026

Published Oct 22, 2026 9:30 Beijing2026-10-22 01:30 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced on October 22, 2026 that it will issue 2-year JGBs in November 2026.

The auction date for this JGB issue is October 29, 2026, the issue date is November 2, 2026, and the maturity date is November 1, 2028.

The issuance size of this 2-year JGB is approximately 2,800 billion yen.
