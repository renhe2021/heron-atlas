Auction Result of 10-Year JGBs on November 5, 2026

Published Nov 5, 2026 11:35 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the November 5, 2026 10-year government bond auction; the issue number is 384, the issue date is November 6, and the maturity date is September 20, 2036, with a coupon rate of 3.1%.

The competitive bid amount was 6,335.5 billion yen, the accepted amount was 1,971.9 billion yen, the lowest accepted price was 102.32 yen per 100 yen, and the yield corresponding to the lowest accepted price was 2.800%.

The allotment ratio at the lowest accepted price was 11.4317%, the weighted average price was 102.57 yen per 100 yen, and the yield corresponding to the average price was 2.768%.

The bid and accepted amount for non-competitive bidding was 1.226 billion yen, the accepted amount for non-price competitive auction I for special participants in the government bond market was 626.6 billion yen, and the accepted amount for non-price competitive auction II was "-".
