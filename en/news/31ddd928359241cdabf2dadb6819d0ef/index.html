Buy-back Results in October 2026

Published Nov 4, 2026 16:00 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance published the results of government bond buybacks for October 2026, listing buyback amounts by security type and issue number.

The buyback amounts for each issue number of 10-year inflation-indexed government bonds in October 2026 were: No. 23 2.6, No. 24 7.1, No. 25 0.1, No. 26 3.8, No. 27 1.4, No. 28 0.1 (billion yen, face value).

Aggregated by quarter, total buybacks were 30.2 billion yen in April-June 2026, 30.1 billion yen in July-September, and 15.1 billion yen in October, for a total of 75.4 billion yen.

A note states that issue No. 31 was not included in the buyback eligible range for April 2026 and from July 2026.
