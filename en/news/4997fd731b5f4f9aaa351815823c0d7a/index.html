ECB wage tracker suggests slightly higher wage growth in first three quarters of 2027 compared with the full year of 2026

Published Nov 4, 2026 08:00 Beijing

First read

The ECB wage tracker indicates that wage growth in the first three quarters of 2027 is slightly higher than in all of 2026.

The tracker incorporates wage agreements signed up to mid-October 2026, with the forward-looking horizon extended to the end of September 2027.

The headline indicator is 2.7% in the second half of 2026 and 2.8% in the first half of 2027, and will fall to 2.4% in the third quarter of 2027 because employee coverage remains limited.

The indicator excluding one-off payments rises from 2.6% in 2026 to 2.8% in the first three quarters of 2027.
