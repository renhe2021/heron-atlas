October 2026 euro area bank lending survey

Published Oct 27, 2026 8:00 Beijing

First read

In the third quarter of 2026, euro-area banks' credit standards for loans to enterprises and consumer credit tightened further on net, driven mainly by increased risk perception and lower risk tolerance; standards for housing loans remained unchanged.

In the same period, demand declined on net across all loan categories, with a net percentage of -6% for enterprise loan demand, -27% for housing loan demand, and -2% for consumer credit demand, with higher interest rates the main dampening factor.

Several banks expect loan demand to finance artificial intelligence infrastructure (especially data centres) to increase gradually but persistently, and current and future AI-related loans are more likely to be directed to infrastructure providers.

Banks' retail funding, debt securities and money market funding channels deteriorated further in the third quarter, with the deterioration in the retail funding channel the largest since the series began in 2011; the reduction in the ECB's monetary policy asset portfolio had a mildly negative impact on banks' market funding conditions and liquidity.
