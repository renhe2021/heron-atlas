Announcement of 20-year JGBs to Be Issued in October 2026

Published Oct 13, 2026 09:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that it will issue 20-year JGBs in October 2026.

The auction date is October 20, 2026, and the issue date is October 21, 2026.

The maturity date of these JGBs is September 20, 2046.

The issue size is approximately 700 billion yen.
