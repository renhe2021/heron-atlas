Piero Cipollone: Interview with Milano Finanza

Published Oct 24, 2026 14:00 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Cipollone said in an interview that the digital euro is intended to fill the gap where cash cannot be used for online payments and to reduce Europe's dependence on non-European card schemes.

The share of online payments in Italy has risen from 6% in 2019 to 24% in 2024, and two-thirds of the relevant card payments in the euro area are processed through non-European schemes.

If the relevant legislation is approved within the year, the ECB will launch a pilot based on real transactions in 2027, and the digital euro could be launched as early as 2029.

The digital euro will have holding limits to safeguard financial stability but will not restrict payment amounts, will support offline transactions, and will be distributed and have customer relationships managed by authorized intermediaries such as banks.
