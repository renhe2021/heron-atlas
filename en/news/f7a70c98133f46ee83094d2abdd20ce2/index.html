8th Conference on Nontraditional Data, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing in Macroeconomics (ECONDAT 2026 Fall Meeting)

Published Oct 5, 2026 8:20 Beijing2026-10-05 00:20 UTC

First read

The Eighth Conference on Non-Traditional Data, Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing in Macroeconomics (ECONDAT 2026 Autumn Meeting) was held from October 5 to 6, 2026, at the Bank of Japan's Tokyo head office.

The conference was hosted by the Bank of Japan and jointly organized with the Bank of Italy, the Federal Reserve Board, Sveriges Riksbank, the Bank of Canada, and the Central Bank Research Association (CEBRA).

The conference aimed to promote research and discussion on data science, non-traditional data, and artificial intelligence technologies including machine learning and natural language processing in the fields of macroeconomics and financial economics.

The agenda covered topics such as measuring sentiment and expectations using large language models, recession forecasting based on big data, and the impact of artificial intelligence adoption on labor markets and financial stability.

InterpretationThe conference focused on large language models parsing central bank communication and market sentiment, the herding effect of AI trading agents and its impact on financial stability; related research may in the future improve the market's interpretation of central bank policy intentions and affect interest rate and foreign exchange volatility.
