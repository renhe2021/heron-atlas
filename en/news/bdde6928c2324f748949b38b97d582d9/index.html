Consumption Activity Index

Published Oct 7, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan's Research and Statistics Department regularly publishes the Consumption Activity Index (CAI), compiled using various sales and supply-side statistics on goods and services, to capture short-term consumption activity on a monthly and quarterly basis.

Private consumption accounts for about 50% of total GDP, so timely and accurate assessment of private consumption trends is essential for judging the macroeconomic cycle.

The CAI provides multiple series, including nominal and real indices, indices with and without adjustment for travel balance, and breakdowns by type of goods and services, and is in principle released at 2:00 p.m. on the fifth business day of each month.
