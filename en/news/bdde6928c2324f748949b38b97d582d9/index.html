Consumption Activity Index

Published Nov 9, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan's Research and Statistics Department regularly publishes research data called the Consumption Activity Index (CAI), which is compiled using supply-side and sales statistics for a variety of goods and services as source data.

The CAI is used to capture short-term consumption activity on a monthly and quarterly basis, and provides nominal and real indices, indices adjusted or not for travel balance, and multiple series classified by type of goods/services.

In principle, the data are released at 14:00 on the fifth business day of each month, but the release schedule may change.

Private consumption accounts for about 50% of total GDP, so timely and accurate assessment of private consumption trends is important for judging the macroeconomic climate.
