Auction Result of 5-Year JGBs on October 14, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Oct 14, 2026 14:15 Beijing2026-10-14 06:15 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the auction results for the 188th 5-year JGBs issued on October 14, 2026.

The coupon rate for this JGB is 2.4%, the maturity date is September 20, 2031, the competitive bid amount was 6,818.2 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 1,915.1 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 100.07 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.384%; the weighted average price was 100.09 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.379%.

The accepted amounts for non-price competitive auctions I and II for JGB market special participants were 583.7 billion yen and 218.8 billion yen, respectively.
