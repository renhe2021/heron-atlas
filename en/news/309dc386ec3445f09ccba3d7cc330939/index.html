Issuance of the Inflation-Indexed Bonds in October-December 2026

Published Sep 29, 2026 16:00 Beijing2026-09-29 08:00 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the issuance and repurchase plan for inflation-indexed bonds for October to December 2026, and noted that the plan may be adjusted due to factors such as market conditions.

The issuance amount of inflation-indexed bonds for November 2026 is approximately 250 billion yen.

The repurchase auction plan for October to December 2026 is: approximately 15 billion yen in October, no implementation in November, and approximately 15 billion yen in December, totaling approximately 30 billion yen, with only one auction held each time, and the eligible issues are No. 22 through No. 30.
