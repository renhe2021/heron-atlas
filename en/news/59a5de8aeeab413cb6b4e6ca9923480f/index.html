Japan Climate Transition Bonds

Published Oct 27, 2026 14:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance released its climate transition bond framework and two second-party opinions, respectively issued in revised versions by JCR and DNV, and both released supplementary assessments confirming alignment with the ICMA Climate Transition Bond Guidelines.

The reporting section includes the allocation and impact report for FY2023 issuances and the allocation report for FY2024 issuances, dated January 2026.

The use of proceeds is for issuances in FY2026 (April 2026 to March 2027), and potential allocation projects are listed.
