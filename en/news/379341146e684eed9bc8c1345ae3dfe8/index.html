Survey on the Access to Finance of Enterprises: lending conditions tightened

Published Oct 26, 2026 17:00 Beijing2026-10-26 09:00 UTC

First read

Euro-area firms reported a further net increase in bank loan interest rates, and the decline in loan availability was larger than the decline in financing demand, causing the financing gap to widen.

Large firms reported interest rate increases more frequently than small and medium-sized enterprises, and their perception that the economic outlook constrains external financing was more pessimistic.

The sample composition of this survey round changed substantially, with first-time respondents accounting for the majority, making the interpretation of numerical items such as selling prices and inflation expectations more challenging.

The 40th round of the SAFE survey was conducted from August 26 to October 2, 2026, covering 12,822 euro-area enterprises, of which 93% had fewer than 250 employees.
