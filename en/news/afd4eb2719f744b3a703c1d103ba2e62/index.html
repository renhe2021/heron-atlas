Bank of Japan Accounts (November 10)

Published Nov 12, 2026 09:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan published its balance sheet as of November 10, 2026, with total assets, total liabilities and net assets both at 625,819,501,896 thousand yen.

On the asset side, JGBs at 504,936,700,731 thousand yen dominate, alongside loans of 68,416,200,000 thousand yen and index-linked ETF trust assets of 36,922,238,339 thousand yen.

On the liability side, current deposits at 413,563,625,559 thousand yen and banknotes at 114,078,530,353 thousand yen dominate, alongside government deposits of 4,284,595,462 thousand yen.

The supplementary table shows that all JGBs are Japanese government bonds, treasury discount bills are 0, and the loan support program balance is 35,664,500,000 thousand yen.
