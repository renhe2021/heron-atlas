Auction Result of 5-Year JGBs on November 12, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Nov 12, 2026 14:15 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the 5-year government bond auction held on November 12, 2026, for special participants in the government bond market.

The issue number of the 5-year government bond is 189, the coupon rate is 2.2%, the issue date is November 13, 2026, and the maturity date is September 20, 2031.

The competitive bid amount was 6,964.6 billion yen, the accepted amount was 1,897.2 billion yen, the lowest accepted price was 100.04 yen per 100 yen, and the yield corresponding to the lowest accepted price was 2.190%.

The weighted average price was 100.06 yen per 100 yen, the yield corresponding to the average price was 2.186%, and the accepted amount in non-price competitive auction I was 602.4 billion yen.
