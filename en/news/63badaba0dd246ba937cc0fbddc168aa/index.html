Revenue of Enterprises above Designated Size in Culture and Related Industries Grows 4.3% in First Three Quarters of 2026

2026年前三季度全国规模以上文化及相关产业企业营业收入增长4.3%

Collected Oct 30, 2026 15:07 Beijing

First read

In the first three quarters of 2026, enterprises above designated size in the culture and related industries nationwide achieved operating revenue of 10,821.0 billion yuan, up 4.3% year on year on a comparable basis.

The 16 small industry categories with relatively obvious characteristics of new cultural formats achieved operating revenue of 5,382.7 billion yuan, up 8.9% year on year, a growth rate 4.6 percentage points faster than that of all culture enterprises above designated size.
