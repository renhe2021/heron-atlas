Piero Cipollone: Shaping the future of money

Published Nov 5, 2026 15:30 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone delivered a keynote speech at the 8th International Conference on Regional Card Schemes and Retail Financial Market Infrastructures, explaining how central bank money can adapt to geopolitical and technological changes.

The Eurosystem adopted a payment strategy covering four objectives and is advancing four initiatives: modernisation of euro banknotes, development of the digital euro, settlement of tokenised asset transactions in central bank money, and interconnection of instant payment systems.

The digital euro legislative process is progressing, and the European Council has called for negotiations to be completed by the end of this year; if legislation is completed by the end of 2026, the goal is to be ready for a first issuance in 2029, with a pilot programme to launch in the second half of 2027 and run for 12 months.

The Eurosystem launched Pontes on 21 September, connecting distributed ledger technology platforms with TARGET services; the Appia project focuses on the architecture, standards and governance of Europe's tokenised financial ecosystem, with the goal of delivering a blueprint in 2028.
