Piero Cipollone: Shaping the future of money

Published Nov 5, 2026 15:30 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone pointed out that the central bank must ensure the currency it issues remains fit for the future, providing a trusted public foundation for payments and finance while leaving room for private innovation.

The Eurosystem has adopted a comprehensive payments strategy comprising four objectives: ensuring central bank money can continue to settle retail and wholesale transactions, strengthening Europe's resilience and strategic autonomy, promoting an integrated and competitive payments ecosystem, and supporting the international role of the euro.

The speech introduced four initiatives: modernizing euro banknotes, developing a digital euro, settling tokenized asset transactions in central bank money (Pontes and Appia), and interlinking instant payment systems.

The digital euro legislative process is advancing; if legislation is completed by the end of 2026, the goal is for the conditions for a first issuance to be in place by 2029; a pilot program is scheduled to launch in the second half of 2027 and run for 12 months.
