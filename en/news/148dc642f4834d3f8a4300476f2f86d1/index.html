Auction Result of 20-Year JGBs on October 20, 2026

Published Oct 20, 2026 11:35 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the auction results for the 198th issue of 20-year Japanese government bonds (JGBs) dated October 20, 2026.

The nominal coupon rate for this issue is 3.8%, the maturity date is September 20, 2046, the competitive bid amount was 3,139.0 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 537.1 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 100.80 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.729%; the weighted average price was 100.87 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.723%.

The accepted amount in non-price competitive auction I for JGB market special participants was 162.8 billion yen, and there was no accepted amount in non-price competitive auction II.
