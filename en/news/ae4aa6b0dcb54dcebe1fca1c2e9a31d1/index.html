International Reserves/Foreign Currency Liquidity (as of the end of September 2026)

Published Oct 7, 2026 07:50 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that as of the end of September 2026, Japan's total reserve assets stood at 1,178,396 million US dollars, a decrease of 29,128 million US dollars from the end of August.

Among official reserve assets, foreign currency reserves were 976,913 million US dollars, of which securities were 821,691 million US dollars and deposits were 155,222 million US dollars; gold was 113,592 million US dollars (27.20 million troy ounces).

Other foreign currency assets were 35,110 million US dollars, including loans to the Japan Bank for International Cooperation (JBIC) totaling 34,817 million US dollars.

Of the scheduled net outflows of short-term foreign currency assets, long positions in foreign currency forwards and futures totaled 20,000 million US dollars.
