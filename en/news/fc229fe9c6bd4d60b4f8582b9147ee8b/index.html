Auction Result of 10-Year JGBs on October 6, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Oct 6, 2026 14:15 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance published the results of the October 6, 2026 auction of 10-year JGBs (Issue No. 384) for special participants in the JGB market.

The coupon rate for this issue is 3.1%, the maturity date is September 20, 2036, the competitive bid amount was 7,401.1 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 1,966.1 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.97 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.103%; the weighted average price was 99.99 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.101%.

The accepted amounts in non-price competitive auctions I and II were 631.5 billion yen and 155.1 billion yen, respectively.
