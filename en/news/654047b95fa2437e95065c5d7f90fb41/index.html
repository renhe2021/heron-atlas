Key Points of the 120th Meeting of JGB Market Special Participants

Published Sep 30, 2026 7:00 Beijing2026-09-29 23:00 UTC

First read

The Debt Management Office proposed maintaining the issuance size of inflation-linked government bonds at 250 billion yen per auction and the buyback size at 15 billion yen for the October to December 2026 quarter.

It proposed reducing the liquidity enhancement auction size for Japanese government bonds with remaining maturities of 5 to 11 years by 150 billion yen from the current level to 500 billion yen, while keeping other maturity segments unchanged.

Most participants supported the proposal, but some expected an increase in the issuance size of inflation-linked government bonds because the breakeven inflation rate has stabilized around 2% and the investor base has expanded.

The Debt Management Office notified that the new floating-rate Japanese government bonds to be first issued after January next year will have a maturity of 2 years, and it plans to announce the issuance terms and details at least two months before the first auction.

InterpretationThe reduction in 5- to 11-year supply and discussion of a possible expansion of inflation-linked government bonds may push down yields in that maturity segment and lift actual inflation expectations; the introduction of a new 2-year floating-rate government bond provides a new pricing anchor for the short-end rate path and is of reference significance for short-end yen interest rates and exchange rate expectations.
