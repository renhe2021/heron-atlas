Initiation of the Investigation of the Anti-Dumping Duty on Pigments and preparations based on rutile titanium dioxide originating in the People’s Republic of China

Published Oct 16, 2026 09:00 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance and Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry announced on October 16, 2026, the decision to initiate a joint anti-dumping duty investigation on rutile-type titanium dioxide pigments and preparations originating from the People's Republic of China.

The investigation was initiated based on an application for anti-dumping duties submitted by the Japan Titanium Dioxide Industry Association to the Minister of Finance on August 7, 2026.

After review, the two ministries determined that there was sufficient evidence to support the initiation, and the investigation will in principle be completed within one year, during which factual investigations will be conducted on Chinese enterprises and domestic producers, and objective evidence will be collected.

Based on the investigation results, the Japanese government will determine, in accordance with WTO agreements and domestic laws and regulations, whether the products in question were imported at dumped prices and caused material injury to the domestic industry, and thereby decide whether to impose anti-dumping duties.
