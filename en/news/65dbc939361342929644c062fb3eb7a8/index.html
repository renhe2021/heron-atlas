Japan's Statement at the 114th Meeting of the Development Committee (Joint Ministerial Committee of the Boards of Governors of the Bank and the Fund) （Bangkok, Thailand –October 15, 2026)

Published Oct 15, 2026 21:30 Beijing

First read

Japan stated at the 114th Development Committee meeting that it was elected chair, and expressed its expectations for the role of the World Bank Group (WBG) amid global geopolitical crises and economic uncertainty.

Japan announced the establishment of the new RISE+ financing window under the World Bank Japan Trust Fund, with an initial contribution of US$20 million for critical minerals supply chains, and pledged USD 40 million to the Global Financing Facility (GFF) to support universal health coverage.

Japan called on all G20 members to participate in the World Bank's Data Sharing Exercise (DSE) to enhance debt transparency, and welcomed the release of the Common Framework memorandum of understanding template to promote more timely debt restructuring.

Japan emphasized that it will continue to support Ukraine in advancing reforms through the IMF program, and encouraged the WBG to use tools such as guarantees and project development to prepare for Ukraine's future reconstruction.
