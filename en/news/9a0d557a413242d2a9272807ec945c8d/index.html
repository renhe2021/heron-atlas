Population Estimates (October 2026 (Reiwa 8) Report)

人口推計（2026年（令和8年）10月報）

Collected Oct 25, 2026 21:37 Beijing

First read

The total population (provisional estimate) as of October 1, 2026 was 122.69 million, a decrease of 530 thousand compared with the same month of the previous year.

The total population (confirmed figure) as of May 1, 2026 was 122.812 million, with the population under 15 and the population aged 15 to 64 decreasing and the population aged 65 and over increasing.

The Japanese national population was 118.838 million, a decrease of 855 thousand compared with the same month of the previous year, while the foreign population was 3.974 million, an increase of 325 thousand compared with the same month of the previous year.
