Auction Result of 5-Year JGBs on November 12, 2026

Published Nov 12, 2026 11:35 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the 5-year JGB (No. 189) auction held on November 12, 2026.

The bond carries a coupon rate of 2.2%, with an issue date of November 13, 2026, and a maturity date of September 20, 2031.

The competitive bid amount was 6,964.6 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 1,897.2 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 100.04 yen per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.190%; the weighted average price was 100.06 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.186%.
