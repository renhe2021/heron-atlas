Survey of Household Economy Results: Expenditure-Related Items: September 2026 (Reiwa 8), July-September 2026 Quarterly Average; ICT-Related Items: July-September 2026 (Reiwa 8) Quarterly Average

家計消費状況調査結果 支出関連項目：2026年（令和8年）9月分、7～9月期平均 ICT関連項目 ：2026年（令和8年）7～9月期平均

Collected Nov 10, 2026 07:41 Beijing

First read

The expenditure-related items of the Survey of Household Economy (spending amounts for specific goods and services and for online shopping) are published monthly, and the September 2026 figures were published on November 10, Reiwa 8, for households of two or more persons.

For the quarterly expenditure-related items, the July-September 2026 average was published on November 10, Reiwa 8, for all households, households of two or more persons, and single-person households.

The ICT-related items (usage of electronic money and purchasing status via the Internet) are published every quarter, and the July-September 2026 average was published on November 10, Reiwa 8, for all households, households of two or more persons, and single-person households.

Because the Survey of Household Economy changed its survey items starting with the January 2021 survey, several result tables also changed starting with the January 2021 survey results.
