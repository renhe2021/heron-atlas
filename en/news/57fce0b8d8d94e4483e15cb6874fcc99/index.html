Survey of Household Economy Results, Expenditure-Related Items: August 2026 (Reiwa 8)

家計消費状況調査結果 支出関連項目：2026年（令和8年）8月分

Collected Oct 25, 2026 21:47 Beijing2026-10-25 13:47 UTC

First read

For the expenditure-related items of the Survey of Household Economy, the monthly results for the August 2026 (Reiwa 8) survey (households of two or more persons) will be published on October 9, Reiwa 8.

In line with the rebasing of the Consumer Price Index to the 2025 base, the real change rates for January 2026 through June 2026 were revised retroactively.

Survey items have been changed since the January 2021 survey, so caution is needed when looking at time-series trends.

The tabulated items include electronic money usage, internet-based purchasing status, expenditure amounts on specific goods and services, and online shopping purchase amounts.

InterpretationThis note is solely an explanation of the release schedule and methodology of Japan's Survey of Household Economy; it contains no actual spending figures or rates of change, and currently has no direct directional implications for the FX or rates markets.
