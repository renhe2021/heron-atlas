Family Income and Expenditure Survey (Households of Two or More Persons: September 2026 (Reiwa 8))

家計調査（二人以上の世帯：2026年（令和8年）9月分）

Collected Nov 10, 2026 07:41 Beijing

First read

The Family Income and Expenditure Survey (households of two or more persons) for September 2026 was published on November 10, 2026, and consumption expenditure for households of two or more persons was 289,536 yen per household, down 6.8% in real terms and down 4.5% in nominal terms year on year.

Month on month (seasonally adjusted values), consumption expenditure (real) fell 2.8%.

Real income of wage-earner households (households of two or more persons) was 528,074 yen per household, up 3.4% in nominal terms year on year, up 0.9% in real terms based on the consumer price index (all items excluding imputed rent of owner-occupied housing), and up 1.3% in real terms based on the consumer price index (all items).

For the 2025 average, consumption expenditure (households of two or more persons) was 314,001 yen per household, up 0.9% in real terms and up 4.6% in nominal terms year on year, and real income of wage-earner households was 653,901 yen per household, up 2.8% in nominal terms year on year.
