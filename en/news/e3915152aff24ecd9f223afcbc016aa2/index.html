Family Income and Expenditure Survey (Households of Two or More Persons: August 2026 (Reiwa 8))

家計調査（二人以上の世帯：2026年（令和8年）8月分）

Collected Oct 25, 2026 21:47 Beijing

First read

Consumption spending for households of two or more persons in August 2026 was 310,975 yen per household, down 3.1% in real terms and down 1.0% in nominal terms year on year.

The actual income of wage-earner households was 616,704 yen per household, up 1.3% in nominal terms year on year, and down 0.6% when deflated by the consumer price index (all items).

Average consumption spending in 2025 was 314,001 yen per household, up 0.9% in real terms year on year, and the actual income of wage-earner households was 653,901 yen per household, up 2.8% in nominal terms year on year.
