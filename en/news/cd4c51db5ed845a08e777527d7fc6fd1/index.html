Report on Internal Migration in Japan, September 2026 (Reiwa 8)

住民基本台帳人口移動報告 2026年（令和8年）9月分

Collected Oct 27, 2026 13:08 Beijing2026-10-27 05:08 UTC

First read

The results of the Report on Internal Migration in Japan for September 2026 (Reiwa 8) were published on October 27, Reiwa 8.

The number of inter-municipal migrants nationwide was 347,332, a decrease of 20,545 (5.6%) compared with the same month of the previous year.

The number of inter-prefectural migrants was 158,887, down 6.9% year on year, and the number of intra-prefectural migrants was 188,445, down 4.4% year on year.

For both Japanese nationals and foreign nationals, the numbers of inter-municipal, inter-prefectural and intra-prefectural migrants all decreased compared with the same month of the previous year.

InterpretationJapan's domestic population migration has declined across the board, with a particularly large drop in cross-prefecture migration, which may reflect weakened labour mobility and insufficient local economic vitality; this is of reference significance for the Bank of Japan in assessing the potential growth rate and inflation pressure, and may dampen expectations for a rise in yen interest rates.
