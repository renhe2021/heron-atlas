Foreign Exchange Intervention Operations (July – September 2026)

Published Nov 9, 2026 07:50 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the status of foreign exchange intervention operations from July to September 2026, with total intervention during the period amounting to 15,399.3 billion yen.

The intervention amount on July 30, 2026 was 7,975.6 billion yen, and the direction of the operation was selling US dollars and buying yen.

The intervention amount on July 31, 2026 was 5,552.3 billion yen, and the intervention amount on August 3, 2026 was 1,871.4 billion yen, with the direction of both operations being selling US dollars and buying yen.
