Indicators for Core CPI

Published Oct 27, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

The core inflation indicators estimated by Bank of Japan Research and Statistics Department staff are obtained by removing temporary disturbances and institutional factors from actual changes in consumer prices, and are used to identify the underlying inflation rate.

The indicators published on this page include the consumer price index excluding institutional factors, as well as the trimmed mean, weighted median, mode, and diffusion index of price changes based on the distribution of price changes.

Institutional factors cover changes in the consumption tax rate, the free education policy (including free school lunches implemented from April 2026), the 2021 mobile phone tariff cuts, the travel subsidy program, and measures to reduce the burden of energy costs.

In principle, the data are released at 2:00 p.m. two business days after the official CPI release, and the latest charts and data were published on October 27, 2026.
