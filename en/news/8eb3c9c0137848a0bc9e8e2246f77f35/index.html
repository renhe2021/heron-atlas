Signing of the Memorandum of Cooperation on Japan-Australia Finance Ministerial Dialogue and the inaugural Japan-Australia Finance Ministers' Dialogue (October 5, 2026)

Published Oct 5, 2026 19:30 Beijing

First read

On October 5, 2026, Japanese Minister of Finance Katayama and Australian Treasurer Jim Chalmers signed the Japan-Australia Finance Ministers' Dialogue Cooperation Memorandum.

Following the signing, the first Japan-Australia Finance Ministers' Dialogue was held in Tokyo, where the two sides exchanged views on topics including the macroeconomic outlook, economic security, investment cooperation and international cooperation.

The ministers of the two countries confirmed their commitment to further strengthening bilateral cooperation.
