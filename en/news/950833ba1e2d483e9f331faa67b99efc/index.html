Developments in Real Exports and Real Imports

Published Oct 22, 2026 15:00 Beijing2026-10-22 07:00 UTC

First read

The Bank of Japan's Research and Statistics Department compiles real export and real import data excluding the effects of price fluctuations, and calculates real exports by region and by goods.

From the April 2026 data onward, the relevant data will, in principle, be released at 4:00 p.m. on the business day following the release of the Ministry of Finance's Trade Statistics (provisional).

These data may be revised retroactively due to revisions to the underlying statistics, base-year changes, or annual revisions, and the compilation method may also be adjusted for economic assessment purposes.

The charts and data are used to inform users interested in economic and financial developments about some of the Bank of Japan's research and analysis.

InterpretationThis page is solely a release note and update notice for the Bank of Japan's research data on actual exports and imports, and does not contain specific figures; subsequently released actual trade data will affect market expectations for Japan's current account and the yen exchange rate.
