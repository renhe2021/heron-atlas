Bank of Japan Accounts (October 20)

Published Oct 22, 2026 9:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan published its balance sheet as of October 20, 2026, with total assets and total liabilities and net assets both at 624,977,993,815 thousand yen.

On the asset side, this mainly includes Japanese government bonds of 504,003,730,754 thousand yen, loans (excluding loans to the Deposit Insurance Corporation) of 68,416,800,000 thousand yen, and index-linked ETF trust assets of 36,942,138,505 thousand yen.

On the liability side, this mainly includes banknotes in circulation of 113,932,904,435 thousand yen and current deposits of 418,348,350,260 thousand yen.

The supplementary table shows a balance of 35,664,500,000 thousand yen for the fund-provisioning measure to promote bank lending under the loan support program, and a loan balance of 32,752,200,000 thousand yen for fund-provisioning operations, etc. provided by way of pooled collateral.
