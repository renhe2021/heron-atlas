Treasury Discount Bills to Be Auctioned on October 16, 2026

Published Oct 9, 2026 09:20 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced that it will auction Treasury discount bills on October 16, 2026.

The issue date is October 19, 2026, and the maturity date is January 25, 2027.

The planned issue amount is approximately 3,300 billion yen, but this amount may be changed, and any change will be announced the day before the auction.
