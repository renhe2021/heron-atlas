Auction Result of 20-Year JGBs on October 20, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Oct 20, 2026 14:15 Beijing2026-10-20 06:15 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance published the results of the auction of 20-year government bonds (Issue No. 198) held on October 20, 2026 for JGB market special participants.

The coupon rate for this issue is 3.8%, the issue date is October 21, and the maturity date is September 20, 2046.

Total competitive bids amounted to 3,139.0 billion yen, total accepted amounted to 537.1 billion yen, and the lowest accepted price was 100.80 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.729%.

The weighted average accepted price was 100.87 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 3.723%; the accepted amounts in non-price competitive auctions I and II were 162.8 billion yen and 54.6 billion yen, respectively.
