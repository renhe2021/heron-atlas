Piero Cipollone: Cash and the digital euro: two sides of the same coin

Published Nov 10, 2026 22:00 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone said the European Parliament adopted its position on the single currency package at its plenary session on July 9, providing strong democratic support for the digital euro.

Cipollone said that swiftly concluding the ongoing trilogue negotiations would pave the way for adoption of the single currency package before the end of the year, thereby providing legal certainty for the digital euro preparatory work and clarifying the possible date of the first issuance.

The digital euro pilot will be launched in the second half of 2027, using a test version without legal tender status, with participants including Eurosystem staff, payment service providers and some merchants.

The ECB plans to make all relevant use cases of the digital euro technically available at the time of first issuance, while market feedback shows that a phased rollout would help ensure a smooth and successful first issuance.
