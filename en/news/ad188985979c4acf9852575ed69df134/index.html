Auction Result of 2-Year JGBs on October 29, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Oct 29, 2026 14:15 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of 2-year government bonds (Issue No. 490) held on October 29, 2026 for JGB market special participants.

The competitive bid amount was 8,539.2 billion yen, the accepted amount was 2,118.4 billion yen, and the lowest accepted price was 99.975 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.912%.

The weighted average price was 99.989 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.905%, and the allocation ratio at the lowest accepted price was 83.6544%.

The amount of non-competitive bids submitted and accepted was 1.070 billion yen, and the accepted amounts in the JGB market special participants' non-price competitive auctions I and II were 680.1 billion yen and 131.8 billion yen, respectively.
