Frank Elderson: Ensuring resilience in the face of uncertainty

Published Oct 14, 2026 8:00 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member and Vice-Chair of the Supervisory Board Frank Elderson delivered a keynote speech at a joint conference of the ECB and the Bank of England, discussing how to ensure the resilience of the financial system in a non-linear world.

The speech noted that since 2022, the average share of interest income that supervised significant banks derive from carbon-intensive sectors has remained above 70%, and that climate and nature risks are forward-looking and difficult to fully quantify using historical data.

Current climate scenario analysis has limitations, and many banks are still at an early stage of incorporating climate and nature risks into their core risk models; stress tests and scenario analyses covered all risk drivers that banks themselves consider material in only about 15% of cases.

In the face of fundamental uncertainty, banks need not only scenario analysis but also to build resilience against unpredictable shocks by maintaining adequate capital and liquidity and improving risk management frameworks.
