Issuance Amounts for Liquidity Enhancement Auctions in October-December 2026

Published Sep 29, 2026 16:00 Beijing2026-09-29 08:00 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the issuance amounts and eligible securities arrangements for liquidity enhancement auctions for October to December 2026.

For the 1- to 5-year remaining maturity category, about 700 billion yen is planned for issuance in November, with no auctions in October and December; for the 5- to 11-year remaining maturity category, about 500 billion yen is planned in each of the three months.

For the 11- to 39-year remaining maturity category, about 250 billion yen is planned in each of October and December, with no auction in November.

The plan may be adjusted due to factors such as market conditions.

InterpretationThe enhanced liquidity auction for Japanese government bonds (JGBs) provides market makers with a channel to replenish off-the-run bonds; regularly announcing issuance sizes helps stabilize the yield curve and improve secondary-market liquidity, and is of reference significance for yen interest rate swaps and cash bond pricing.
