Regional Economic Report (Summary) (Oct. 2026)

Published Oct 8, 2026 13:00 Beijing2026-10-08 05:00 UTC

First read

The Bank of Japan released a summary of its Regional Economic Report on October 8, 2026, compiling the economic assessments of its branches for Japan's nine regions.

All nine regions reported that their economies were recovering moderately, improving, or improving moderately, although some regions showed weakness.

Compared with the July 2026 assessment, the Tohoku and Shikoku regions upgraded their wording on the pace of improvement, the Kyushu-Okinawa region adjusted its wording due to the impact of the 2026 Kumamoto earthquake, and the remaining regions remained unchanged.

The assessment for the Kanto-Koshinetsu region noted that some of the weakness was related to the impact of the situation in the Middle East.
