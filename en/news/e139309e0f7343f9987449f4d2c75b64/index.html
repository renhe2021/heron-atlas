Appointments to BIS global payments standard-setting body and Consultative Council for the Americas

Published Nov 10, 2026 08:00 Beijing

First read

Bank of Korea Governor Hyun Song Shin has been appointed Chair of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI) for a three-year term starting November 1.

In addition, the Board has extended Bank of Canada Governor Tiff Macklem's term as Chair of the Consultative Council for the Americas (CCA) until March 31, 2027.
