Frank Elderson: Taking action for nature – investing in economic resilience and prosperity

Published Oct 26, 2026 13:00 Beijing

First read

ECB Executive Board member Frank Elderson delivered a keynote speech on Finance and Biodiversity Day at COP17, stressing that protecting natural capital is an investment in economic resilience and future prosperity.

ECB analysis shows that 72% of firms in the euro area depend on at least one ecosystem service, and 75% of corporate loans go to companies highly dependent on ecosystem services.

The speech noted that extreme weather already poses a material risk to the euro-area economy, with once-in-a-century droughts potentially putting up to 24% of euro-area economic output at risk and nearly 12% of economic output at risk from inadequate flood protection.

In banking supervision, the ECB requires banks to identify and manage climate- and nature-related risks, and it has explicitly incorporated nature risk factors in its recent monetary policy assessments and communication.
