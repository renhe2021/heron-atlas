Auction Result of Treasury Discount Bills on November 9, 2026

Published Nov 9, 2026 11:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the auction results for Treasury Discount Bills issue number 1418, dated November 9, 2026.

The auction date for this issue was November 9, 2026, the issue date was November 10, 2026, and the maturity date is May 10, 2027.

Competitive bid amount was 8,433.20 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 2,319.58 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.310 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.4011%; the weighted average price was 99.318, corresponding to a yield of 1.3847%.
