Japan Bank for International Cooperation(JBIC) EUR Bonds guaranteed by Japan (the ''Bonds'')

Published Sep 30, 2026 16:00 Beijing2026-09-30 08:00 UTC

First read

The Japanese government provides a guarantee for euro-denominated bonds issued by the Japan Bank for International Cooperation (JBIC).

The bonds will be issued on October 8, 2026, with an issue size of 1.5 billion euros, a coupon rate of 4.000%, and a yield of 4.009%.

The bonds mature on October 7, 2033, and the issue price is 99.946%.
