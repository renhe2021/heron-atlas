U.S. International Trade in Goods and Services, September 2026

Published Nov 4, 2026 21:30 Beijing

First read

Data from the US Bureau of Economic Analysis and the US Census Bureau show that the US goods and services trade deficit narrowed in September 2026.

The deficit fell to $105.0 billion in September from $105.7 billion in August (revised), as exports rose more than imports.

The September goods deficit decreased by $0.7 billion to $135.9 billion.

The September services surplus decreased by less than $0.1 billion to $30.9 billion.
