IMES Discussion Paper Series (2026)

Published Oct 9, 2026 10:00 Beijing

First read

The Bank of Japan's Institute for Monetary and Economic Studies (IMES) released its list of 2026 discussion paper series, aimed at promoting academic discussion and comment.

The views expressed in the papers are those of the authors personally and do not necessarily reflect the positions of the Bank of Japan or IMES.

A total of 13 papers were released in 2026, covering fields such as economics, finance and information technology, with topics including the impact of electrification on productivity, new perspectives on monetary policy, loan pricing, optical quantum computing, central bank independence, the real effects of nominal interest rates, recession forecasting, shadow short-term interest rate models, computerization and routine work, dispersion in interbank market loan rates, determinants of liquidity in the Japanese government bond market, exit from low interest rates, and employment protection and labour market mobility.
