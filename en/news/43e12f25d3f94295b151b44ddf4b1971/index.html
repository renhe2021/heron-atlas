Auction Result of Treasury Discount Bills on October 23, 2026

Published Oct 23, 2026 11:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance on October 23, 2026 announced the auction results for the 1415th issue of Treasury discount bills.

The issue date for this bill is October 26, and the maturity date is February 1, 2027; the competitive bid amount was 977.140 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 254.738 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.6585 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.2762%, and the weighted average price was 99.6607, corresponding to a yield of 1.2680%.

The non-competitive auction I subscription amount by special participants in the government bond market was 75.260 billion yen.
