Auction Result of 10-Year JGBs on November 5, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Nov 5, 2026 14:15 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the 10-year government bond auction held on November 5, 2026, for special participants in the government bond market.

The issue number of this government bond is 384, with a coupon rate of 3.1%, an issue date of November 6, 2026, and a maturity date of September 20, 2036.

The competitive bid amount was 6,335.5 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 1,971.9 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 102.32 yen per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.800%; the weighted average price was 102.57 yen, corresponding to a yield of 2.768%.
