Auction Result of 2-Year JGBs on September 30, 2026 (For JGB Market Special Participants)

Published Sep 30, 2026 14:15 Beijing2026-09-30 06:15 UTC

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance announced the results of the auction of 2-year government bonds (No. 489) held on September 30, 2026 for special participants in the JGB market.

The competitive bid amount was 8,344.8 billion yen, the accepted amount was 2,145.3 billion yen, the lowest accepted price was 100.055, corresponding to a yield of 1.971%.

The weighted average accepted price was 100.069, corresponding to a yield of 1.964%, and the allotment ratio at the lowest accepted price was 39.3473%.

The non-competitive bid subscription and accepted amount was 0.079 billion yen, and the accepted amounts in non-price competitive auctions I and II for special participants were 654.0 billion yen and 228.9 billion yen, respectively.
