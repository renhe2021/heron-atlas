Retail Price Survey: Retail Prices of Major Items in the Ku-area of Tokyo and Prices of Nationally Uniform-Priced Items (October 2026)

小売物価統計調査 主要品目の東京都区部小売価格及び全国統一価格品目の価格（2026年10月分）

Collected Oct 30, 2026 7:40 Beijing

First read

This page presents the results of the Retail Price Survey (Trend Edition), publishing the retail prices in the Ku-area of Tokyo for major items and the prices for items with a nationwide uniform price; the prices in the survey results are tax-inclusive prices.

Monthly results have been published since January 2000 (Heisei 12), and annual results since 2001 (Heisei 13).

Regarding "city-by-city retail prices of gasoline," the February 2026 results will be the last, and advance publication will be discontinued; for results from March 2026 onward, please refer to "city-by-city retail prices of major items."

The page provides links to statistical tables for each year and month, and lists explanations concerning changes to the statistical tables and database integration.
