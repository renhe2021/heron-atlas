Personal Income and Outlays, September 2026

Published Oct 29, 2026 20:30 Beijing

First read

The US Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) estimates that personal income increased by $54.5 billion in September 2026, a 0.2% increase at a monthly rate.

Disposable personal income (DPI) increased by $52.7 billion, or 0.2%; personal consumption expenditures (PCE) increased by $113.1 billion, or 0.5%.

Personal outlays (the sum of PCE, personal interest payments and personal current transfer payments) increased by $113.0 billion in September.

Personal saving was $911.1 billion, and the personal saving rate (personal saving as a percentage of DPI) was 3.8%.
