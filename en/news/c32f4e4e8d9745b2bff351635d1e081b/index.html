Auction Result of Treasury Discount Bills on October 9, 2026

Published Oct 9, 2026 11:30 Beijing

First read

Japan's Ministry of Finance on October 9, 2026 announced the auction results for the 1412th issue of Treasury discount bills.

The issue date of these bills is October 13, 2026, the maturity date is January 18, 2027, the competitive bid amount was 11,575.40 billion yen, and the accepted amount was 2,529.66 billion yen.

The lowest accepted price was 99.6610 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.2799%, and the weighted average price was 99.6626 per 100 yen, corresponding to a yield of 1.2738%.

The amount subscribed in non-competitive auction I for special participants in the government bond market was 770.30 billion yen.
