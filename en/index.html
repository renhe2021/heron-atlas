HERON ATLAS / FIELD NOTES
PRIMARY FACTS · STEADY WATCH

WHY HERON ATLAS

We do the daily macro homework first.

The problem

  • Official information is spread over dozens of websites, in English, Japanese and Chinese, each with its own time conventions.
  • Wire headlines often give no source and mix facts with opinion, so they are hard to check.
  • When a central bank decides, when key data come out and what the market expects take several places to look up.

What we do

  • We follow official releases from central banks, statistics offices and finance ministries, checking every 30 minutes.
  • Each release gets a first read: what it says, the key numbers quoted as published, and a single link to the official original.
  • An economic calendar in Beijing time with a countdown to key events, which you can subscribe to.

Why us

  • Primary sources: every item links back to the official original.
  • One format and one clock, so you do not translate or convert anything yourself.
  • Transparent: every item links to its official original and shows when it was published; first-read pages say they are model-generated.
  • 18official sources, checked every 30 minutes
  • 12official releases in the past 24 hours
  • 40recent releases readable in English
